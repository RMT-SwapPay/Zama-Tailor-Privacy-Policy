## Zama-Tailor-Privacy-Policy effective from November 01 2026
Zama Tailor

## Who we are

Zama Tailor is a shop and customer app for measurements, orders, portfolios, and messages between a customer and a tailor.

## Data we store

We store your name, email, phone number, profile photo, measurement profiles, orders, shop details, portfolio and newsfeed photos, chat messages, reviews, and a notification token for this device.

## How it is used

Your account lets you sign in. Measurements are private until you publish a share code or save them to the phone number on your profile, which a tailor can look up when creating an order. Orders track progress and payment records. Messages stay between you and the other person in the chat. Photos appear on your profile, portfolio, or posts.

## Who can see it

Shop name, city, staff count, available slots, portfolio, posts, and reviews are visible in the directory. A measurement share code can be imported by any signed-in tailor who has the code. Phone lookup is available to signed-in tailors who enter that exact number.

## How long we keep it

We keep this data while your account exists. Delete My Account removes your profile, measurements, share codes, messages, notification records, and photos. Orders already placed keep a ledger entry under the name Deleted user so the shop record stays complete.

## Advertising

When ads are turned on, Google AdMob may show ads in the app. Google can use a device advertising id, IP address, and general location to choose and measure those ads. You can limit ad tracking in your phone settings. Full-screen ads appear only from actions in the app, not while you are filling a form.

## Account deletion

Open Profile or Shop and choose Delete my account. You will be asked to sign in again, then the app calls the account deletion service. This is available inside the app.
